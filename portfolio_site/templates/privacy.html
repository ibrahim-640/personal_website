Privacy Policy

How we handle your data

This policy explains what personal data we collect through this website, why we collect it and what your rights are.

Last updated: 17 October 2026

  1. Who we are

    MUTITECH is a business based in Nairobi, Kenya. We decide how and why the data described here is used. You can reach us at mwitaibrahim88@gmail.com.

  2. What we collect

    • When you send a request through our form: your name, email address, chosen package, your project details, your confirmation that you accepted our Terms of Service, and the date and time of the request.
    • When you contact us by WhatsApp or email: the details you send us.
    • Technical details, such as your IP address and browser type, which our hosting provider's server logs record automatically.

  3. Why we use it

    • To reply to your request and send you a quote.
    • To keep a record of what you agreed to.
    • To run the website, keep it secure and fix problems.

    We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

  4. Who else handles it

    We use other services to run the site. Our hosting provider stores the website and its database. An email service delivers the notification of your request to us. Our pages load fonts from Google, so your browser contacts Google when you visit. These services process data on our behalf, or in the case of fonts, receive your IP address from your browser.

  5. Cookies

    We only use cookies that the site needs to work, such as a security cookie that protects our contact form. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

  6. The chatbot

    Our chat assistant runs in your browser and answers from fixed information about our packages. The messages you type into it are not sent to us or stored.

  7. How long we keep your data

    We keep request details for up to 24 months after our last contact with you, then delete them. For clients, we keep project records for as long as we need them to support your project and to meet legal and accounting obligations.

  8. Your rights

    Under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019, you may ask to see the data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete it, and object to how we use it. Email us and we will respond promptly. You may also complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner in Kenya.

  9. Keeping data safe

    We use reasonable measures to protect your data, including encrypted connections and restricted access to our admin area. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

  10. Children

    This website is for businesses and adults, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

  11. Changes to this policy

    We may update this policy and will change the date at the top when we do. If we start using analytics tools or a different chat assistant, we will update this page first.

  12. Contact

    Questions about your data? Email mwitaibrahim88@gmail.com or message us on WhatsApp.
