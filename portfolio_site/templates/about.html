  1. About these terms

    These terms apply to every project MUTITECH ("we", "us") delivers for a client ("you"). They become an agreement between us when you accept a quote in writing or pay the deposit, whichever comes first.

  2. Packages and quotes

We do not guarantee that any AI feature will always be accurate or available.
